I want to design a really high level LLM orchestrator. The idea here is that we can keep sub agent scopes relatively small and avoid compaction from high level tasks. My goals is for this to be something you can give your API keys this whole system will work for any project. For me this will be specific to research and should mimic a research lab in form.

The idea is for there be a top level manager (likely running the best model available) that talks to sub agents who are managers of their respective departments. In these departments the managers can create sub agents to work on specific tasks. The managers never go away but they also need to comfortably resume work if they do go away. The main teams are research, tooling, experimentation, and verification (I am open to there being more, this is just a first go of the idea). The research team handles the big theoretical ground work for what is possible, what technical direction should we head in, etc. They will do surveys on the internet, look at research papers, and look at our experimental results to decide what makes sense. The tooling team creates high quality tools for any team to use. This minimizes the amount of work that needs to be repeated. They create and amend existing tools based on the needs of the overall project. The experiment team is responsible for conducting experiments. They make sure that we are talking to the hardware we need to talk to, able to gather the data we need to gather, and try to automate but oversee things occurring. The verification teams job is to take specific task results and compare them to the broader scope. They inform managers on how things are going so that managers can correct course to stay on task. They look at the things with lots of scrutiny and strictness to make sure high level goals are being work toward.

There is also a communication/ticketing system. The ticketing system is meant for a job to come from top level manager, to department manager, to worker, to possibly lower tier worker. The tickets can be split and delegated if the managers/ top-level works thing that makes sense. The ticket should have a path trace of where it came from and contain the context that the specific agent should have to accomplish its task.
